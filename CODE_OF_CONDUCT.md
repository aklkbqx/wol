# Community conduct

Be respectful, specific and constructive. Welcome newcomers and differing
experience levels. Discuss technical choices without personal attacks.

Harassment, threats, discrimination and publishing someone's private information
are not acceptable. Maintainers may edit or remove harmful content and restrict
participation, explaining the reason when practical.

For a conduct concern, contact the maintainer through the contact options on
https://github.com/aklkbqx. Request a private channel before sending sensitive
details. Do not post personal information in public issues.
